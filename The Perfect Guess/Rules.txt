🎮 Game Rules ---

Choose a difficulty level (Easy, Medium, or Hard).

The computer will generate a random number in the selected range.

Players take turns to guess the number.

After each guess, the computer will give a hint:

"Higher number please" → if your guess is too low.

"Lower number please" → if your guess is too high.

The total number of attempts is counted for each player.

The player who guesses the number in fewer attempts wins.

If both players need the same number of attempts, the match is a draw.
